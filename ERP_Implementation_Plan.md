# ERP System & Interactive Dashboard: Implementation Proposal for U:DO Craft

This document outlines the strategic roadmap for expanding the U:DO Craft ecosystem into a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform. By integrating these modules with the existing Dashboard, we will create a unified operational hub for production, sales, and financial management.

---

## 📊 1. Interactive Dashboard (Interactive Panel)
The central nerve center of the application, providing real-time visibility into the health of the business.

- **Real-time Key Metrics Visualization:**
    - Live tracking of Net Revenue, Gross Profit, and Average Order Value (AOV).
    - Dynamic widgets for "Low Stock" alerts and "Urgent Production Orders."
    - Real-time visitor counts and conversion funnel metrics.
- **Real-time Data Analytics:**
    - Interactive charts showing sales trends (daily/weekly/monthly).
    - Geographical distribution of sales.
    - Marketing channel effectiveness (ROI/ROAS).

---

## 📦 2. Warehouse (Склад)
Comprehensive inventory management to ensure production never stops.

- **Product Control:** SKU management with variant tracking (color, size, material).
- **Stock Movements:** Automated logs for every item entry and exit.
- **Inventory Tracking:** multi-warehouse support (Raw Materials vs. Finished Goods).
- **Operations Management:** Mobile-friendly interface for warehouse staff to perform "Pick & Pack" operations using barcode scanners.

---

## 👥 3. Counterparties (Контрагенти)
A centralized CRM and SRM (Supplier Relationship Management) system.

- **Buyer and Supplier Profiles:** Detailed contact info, payment history, and credit limits.
- **Statistical Reports:** Ranking of top customers by LTV and top suppliers by reliability/pricing.
- **Sales Funnel:** Visual tracking of leads from initial contact to repeat customer.

---

## 💰 4. Sales (Продажі)
From lead generation to fiscal compliance.

- **Performance Dashboard:** Visualizing individual manager performance vs. targets.
- **Order Registry:** Enhanced filtering for status, payment, and delivery (Nova Poshta/Global).
- **Invoice & PRRO Integration:** Automated invoice generation and synchronization with Software Fiscal Receipt services (e.g., Checkbox/Вчасно).
- **Marketplace Sync:** Seamless integration with KeyCRM, Etsy, and Shopify.

---

## 🛒 5. Purchases (Закупки)
Optimizing the supply chain.

- **Procurement Planning:** Automated "Low Stock" reports that generate draft Purchase Orders.
- **Supply Chain Management:** Tracking raw material lead times and supplier performance.

---

## 💳 6. Financial Operations (Фінанси)
Full control over cash flow and settlements.

- **Payment Tracking:** Integration with Monobank/Privat24 for automatic transaction matching.
- **Cash Flow Monitoring:** Daily/Weekly/Monthly reports on inflows vs. outflows.
- **Payment Calendar:** Visibility into upcoming liabilities and receivables to prevent cash gaps.
- **Counterparty Settlements:** Automated reconciliation of debts and prepayments.

---

## 🏭 7. Production (Виробництво)
The core of the "Craft" business.

- **Production Cycle Tracking:** Monitoring every stage: Cutting -> Sewing -> Printing -> Quality Control -> Packing.
- **Prime Cost Calculation:** Automated allocation of material costs, labor (Salary), and overheads to calculate the true margin of each product.

---

## 📅 8. Tasks & Salary (Завдання та Зарплата)
Human resource and workflow optimization.

- **Task Planning:** Kanban boards for production and administrative tasks.
- **Work Time Scheduling:** Digital "Time Cards" for staff.
- **Salary Calculations:** Automated accruals based on "Piecework" (per item produced) or fixed hourly rates.

---

## 🏢 9. Fixed Assets & Directories (Основні засоби та Довідники)
System infrastructure and non-inventory assets.

- **Fixed Assets:** Tracking equipment (sewing machines, printers) depreciation and maintenance schedules.
- **Master Data (Directories):** Unified registries for all base entities (Price lists, Currencies, Logistics providers).

---

## 🚀 Implementation Priority
1.  **Phase 1:** Core Warehouse expansion & PRRO integration (Sales).
2.  **Phase 2:** Financial Operations & Payment integration.
3.  **Phase 3:** Production Cycle & Prime Cost tracking.
4.  **Phase 4:** Tasks, Salary, and Fixed Assets.
